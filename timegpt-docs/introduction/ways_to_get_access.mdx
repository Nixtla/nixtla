## Enterprise trial

If you're a company evaluating the Nixtla Engine and TimeGPT, tell us about your use case and we'll set up a 30-minute call with our forecasting experts. We'll look at your data and goals, recommend the right setup, and activate your trial after the call.

Trials are available through the API, or as a self-hosted deployment for teams with data residency or infrastructure requirements.

[**Request free trial →**](https://www.nixtla.io/book-a-free-trial?utm_source=nixtla.io&utm_campaign=/docs/introduction/ways_to_get_access)

## Existing customers

If your team already has a Nixtla contract, sign in to the dashboard to manage API keys and check usage. For questions about your plan, contact your account team.

To sign in, find the dashboard link your account team sent you by email.

## Free academic access

If you're a student or researcher, sign up with your university email and get an API key right away. We'll grant you access for 30 days, but if you tell us what you're working on and cite Nixtla in your research, we can extend it for up to 2 years.

[**Request academic access →**](https://www.nixtla.io/researchers-and-students)
